# Week 6 – Business Trend Analysis Using Tableau

## Project Overview

This project analyzes monthly business sales trends using Tableau Public. An Area Chart is used to understand changes in monthly sales over time, along with additional visualizations for department sales, employee gender distribution, and the relationship between annual salary and monthly sales.

## Dataset

- Dataset: Statistics Practice Employee Sales Dataset
- File Format: CSV
- Total Records: 180 employees
- Time Field: Join Date
- Business Measure: Monthly Sales K
- Tool Used: Tableau Public

## Visualizations

1. Monthly Sales Trend – Area Chart
2. Total Sales by Department – Bar Chart
3. Employee Distribution by Gender – Pie Chart
4. Annual Salary vs Monthly Sales – Scatter Plot

## Tableau Public Dashboard

[View the Business Trend Analysis Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/views/BusinessTrendAnalysisMonthlySalesDashboard/BusinessTrendAnalysisMonthlySalesDashboard?:language=en-US&:sid=&:redirect=auth&:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link)

## Business Insights

1. February recorded the highest monthly sales, with approximately 5.6K.
2. July was the second-highest performing month, with approximately 5.3K in sales.
3. May recorded the lowest monthly sales, followed closely by October.
4. Finance generated the highest departmental sales of 10,120, while HR recorded the lowest sales of 8,975.
5. Female, Male, and Other employee groups are equally represented, with 60 employees in each group.
6. The scatter plot shows variations in monthly sales across different annual salary levels and departments.

## Conclusion

The dashboard shows that monthly sales fluctuate throughout the year. February and July are the strongest sales months, while May and October require additional business attention. Finance is the highest-performing department. The business can study successful strategies used during peak months and apply them during lower-performing periods.
